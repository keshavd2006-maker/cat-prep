Lesson 1: Percentages

Percent means per hundred. x% of y = (x/100) × y. Percentages sit under profit and loss, interest and data interpretation, so this lesson pays off everywhere.

Memorise these

1/2 = 50%1/3 = 33.33%1/4 = 25%1/5 = 20%1/6 = 16.67%1/7 = 14.28%1/8 = 12.5%1/9 = 11.11%1/10 = 10%1/11 = 9.09%

Multiples follow: 3/8 = 3 × 12.5 = 37.5%.

Six techniques

  1. Percentage change = (New − Old) ÷ Old × 100. The base is always the original value.
  2. Successive changes: a% then b% gives a + b + ab/100. Up 20% then down 20% is 20 − 20 − 4 = −4%.
  3. A more than B: if A is x% more than B, then B is x/(100+x) × 100 percent less than A. For x = 25 that is 20%.
  4. Price and consumption: if price rises x%, cut consumption by x/(100+x) × 100 percent to keep spending equal.
  5. Multipliers: +20% is ×1.2, −20% is ×0.8, +10% is ×1.1, −10% is ×0.9. Multiply them all. Fastest method in the exam.
  6. Ask "percent of what?" Most CAT percentage errors come from the wrong base.

Worked example

A student scores 40% and fails by 30 marks. At 50% she would pass by 20 marks. Find the maximum marks.

The gap between 50% and 40% is 30 + 20 = 50 marks. So 10% of the total is 50, and the total is 500.
